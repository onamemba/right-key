How it works
A pretrained neural net (Spotify Basic Pitch) hears the audio and writes down every note — pitch and timing. Then plain music theory takes over: the notes vote on the key, nearby notes get grouped into chords, and when a chord loop repeats it counts as "learned". Sing mode hears your melody, figures out the key, and invents chords that fit underneath it.

Best on piano, guitar, or singing. Full band mixes confuse it — drums have no pitch and the bass fights the chords.
